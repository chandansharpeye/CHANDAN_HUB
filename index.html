90%+ Exam Strategy

No plan can promise marks, but these habits stack the odds in your favour. Follow them in this order.

My exam countdown

Pick your exam date to see what to focus on today.
Know the paperStudy the syllabus, marking scheme and weightage first. Spend most time on high-weightage chapters.
Textbook firstFinish the main textbook and its exercises before any guide or extra material.
Active recallClose the book and write or say what you remember. Re-reading feels easy but teaches less.
Spaced revisionRevise on day 1, 3, 7 and 21. Short and frequent beats one long cram.
Past papersSolve the last 5 to 10 years' papers in exam time. Check with the marking scheme, not just the answer.
Error notebookWrite each mistake with the correct method. Redo it after 3 days until it never repeats.
Write for marksUse headings, key terms, steps and neat diagrams. Marks are given for steps, not only the final answer.
Exam dayUse the first 10 minutes to read and plan. Start with your strongest questions, watch time per mark, and keep 10 minutes to check.
Body and mindSleep 7 hours, eat on time and take a short walk. A rested brain remembers more.

Commerce Corner

Key ideas from Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics at a glance.

Golden rules of accounting

  • Personal: debit the receiver, credit the giver.
  • Real: debit what comes in, credit what goes out.
  • Nominal: debit expenses and losses, credit incomes and gains.

Three subjects, three questions

  • Accountancy: how do we record money?
  • Business Studies: how is a business run?
  • Economics: how do choices shape markets?
